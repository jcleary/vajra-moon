A taste of traditional Buddhist thought and practice

Vajra moon is open to everybody. No matter your faith, background or how little or how much you know about Buddhism, you are welcome.

The session consists of a brief meditation session which will be explained beforehand, then a short talk on Buddhist thought, followed by a shorter meditation session. Afterwards there is opportunity for discussion and free coffee, tea and snacks.

Questions are welcome. You don't have to agree with what you hear; questioning and stress-testing what the Buddha taught is what he encouraged his students to do, and is a vibrant element of the Buddhist tradition!

Run by the oldest Buddhist centre in Manchester

Vajra Moon is organised by Kagyu Ling, an authentic Tibetan Buddhist centre in Chorlton that has been active for over 50 years, under the spiritual authority of Khaywang Lama Jampa Thaye and his Guru, Karma Thinley Rinpoche.
